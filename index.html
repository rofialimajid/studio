Questions

FAQ

A few things to know before choosing a template.

What kinds of templates do you make?

Rofi Studio creates templates across different creative formats, from websites and portfolios to books, email, and other digital products. The collection continues to grow over time.

What do I get?

You get the template files and the assets included in the individual product. Each product page explains exactly what is included.

Can I customize the templates?

Yes. You can adapt the content, typography, images, colors, links, layout, and other editable details depending on the product.

Are the templates easy to edit?

Templates are designed to provide a clear starting point and make customization straightforward. The editing process depends on the format and tools used by each product.

Where can I buy them?

Templates are sold through Gumroad. Use the Shop on Gumroad button on each product card to view the product details and purchase it.
